Drop album tracks here:

  01-track.flac
  02-track.flac
  03-track.mp3
  ...

Or any supported audio format (.flac .mp3 .ogg .opus .wav .m4a)

Optional menu/ subfolder:
  menu/cover.png
  menu/theme.ogg

Rename "Example Album" and update cartridge.json.

The "path" in json can be the folder (ends with /) — all tracks inside are the album.
