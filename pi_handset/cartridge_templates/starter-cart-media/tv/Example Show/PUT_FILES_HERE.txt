TV SHOW LAYOUT
==============

  menu/           bg.png, theme.ogg (show menu)
  s01/            season 1 episodes
    e01.mkv
    e02.mkv
  s02/
    e01.mkv
    e02.mkv

"autoplay": true in cartridge.json → plays next episode automatically.

Rename "Example Show" folder and update paths in cartridge.json.

Episode titles in json are what Digivice shows in the list.
