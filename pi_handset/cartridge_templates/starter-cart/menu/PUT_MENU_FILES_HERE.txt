Optional — shared menu for the whole cart (DVD-style):

  bg.png         background image
  theme.ogg      looping menu music
  select.wav     sound when you move the cursor

In cartridge.json add:

  "menu": {
    "background": "menu/bg.png",
    "music": "menu/theme.ogg",
    "select_sound": "menu/select.wav"
  }
